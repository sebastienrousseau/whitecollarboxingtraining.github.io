## Conformance Status

`whitecollarboxingtraining.com` is engineered and audited against the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AAA**:

- **Colour Contrast (>= 7.0:1)**: Body copy, headings, links, and interactive controls meet or exceed a 7:1 contrast ratio in both Light and Dark themes.
- **Keyboard &amp; Focus Navigation**: Every interactive control supports keyboard navigation with a high-contrast 3px focus ring and a skip-to-main-content link.
- **Zoom &amp; Reflow**: Pinch-to-zoom is unrestricted and layouts reflow cleanly down to 320px CSS width without horizontal scrolling.
