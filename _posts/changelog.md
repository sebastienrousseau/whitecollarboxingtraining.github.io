## v0.0.2 (2026-11-01)

- **SSG &amp; Skeletonic CSS Port**: Ported `whitecollarboxingtraining.github.io` from the legacy 2015 Google App Engine Python 2.7 / Jinja2 application to a static site generated by Rust `ssg` and Skeletonic CSS with WCAG 2.2 Level AAA contrast (`>= 7.0:1`) in both light and dark modes.
- **Legacy Archive**: Preserved the complete 2015 Google App Engine codebase under `archive/legacy-gae-2015/` while maintaining backwards-compatible `.html` routes (`/story.html`, `/whats-involved.html`, `/trainers.html`, `/classes.html`, `/get-in-touch.html`).
- **SEO, Google Search &amp; AI Search Optimisation**: Added Schema.org `@graph` JSON-LD (`ExerciseGym`, `SportsActivityLocation`, `LocalBusiness`, `Person` for Gary Foley and Sean Murphy, `OfferCatalog`), `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `agents.txt`, `.well-known/ai-plugin.json`, `.well-known/mcp.json`, and WebP responsive assets.
- **Cloudflare Edge Configuration**: Configured `wrangler.toml`, `_headers`, `_redirects`, and `CNAME` for `whitecollarboxingtraining.com`.

## v0.0.1 (2026-11-01)

- Enforced HTTPS URLs in `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` and added dual `Apache-2.0 OR MIT` licensing.
