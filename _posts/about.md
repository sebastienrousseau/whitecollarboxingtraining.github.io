## Our Commitment to Boxing Excellence

**White Collar Boxing Training** provides structured boxing tuition for executives, professionals, adults, and youth at **Anchor Hall, Bulwer Rd, Barnet, London EN5 5EX**.

Our training aligns with the standards of the **International White Collar Boxing Association**, prioritising:
- **Progressive Skill Mastery**: Three defined tiers (`Basic / Beginner`, `Amateur / Mid-Level`, and `Advanced`).
- **Experienced Supervision**: Every session is overseen by qualified coaches Gary Foley and Sean Murphy.
- **Inclusive Digital Experience**: Our website is engineered with the Rust `static-site-generator` (`ssg`) and Skeletonic CSS to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AAA accessibility standards.
