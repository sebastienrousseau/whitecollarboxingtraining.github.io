Privacy Policy

How White Collar Boxing Training protects your privacy online and when you enquire about training in Barnet, London.

Zero Tracking Cookies

whitecollarboxingtraining.com is a static website built with Rust ssg. Unlike the retired 2015 site, we do not load Facebook Login widgets, AddThis social trackers, Google+ beacons, or third-party advertising cookies.

The only browser storage used is an optional localStorage key (theme) if you manually switch between Light and Dark mode.

Direct Telephone & Coaching Enquiries

When you call our trainers on 07749 116927 or 07729 980677, your contact details are used solely to schedule your boxing sessions at Anchor Hall, Bulwer Rd, Barnet, London EN5 5EX, and are never sold or shared with third-party marketers.
