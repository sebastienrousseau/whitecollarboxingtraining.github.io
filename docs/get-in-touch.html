How to Find Anchor Hall, Barnet (EN5 5EX)

  • By London Underground (Northern Line): Alight at High Barnet station (Zone 5, Northern Line). Walk north up Barnet Hill / High Street and turn east toward Bulwer Road (approximately 10–12 minutes on foot).
  • By National Rail: Alight at New Barnet station (Great Northern services from Moorgate, Old Street, Highbury & Islington, and Finsbury Park). Anchor Hall on Bulwer Road is an 8-minute walk west from New Barnet station.
  • By Bus & Road: Served by Barnet bus routes along Station Road and Great North Road (A1000), with convenient access from the M25 (Junction 23 / 24) and A1.
